# Editing the Calvary website

This is the office guide. You change the words on the site from a form called Tina. You do not need to write code, and you do not need Grok.

Sermon video, the church app, and online giving stay in **Subsplash**. Tina does not replace those.

## Where to open it

On this computer, during setup, open:

[http://127.0.0.1:4321/admin/index.html](http://127.0.0.1:4321/admin/index.html)

That address only works while the site is running here. When the office can open Tina from anywhere, Aaron will replace this address. Until then, ask Aaron to start it.

One person should edit at a time. Sign out when you are done.

## What you will see

The page is on the left. The form is on the right.

Click a sentence on the page. The matching box opens in the form, and the page changes while you type. You are looking at a preview. The public website does not change until the update is saved and published.

These parts of the page can be clicked:

- The large homepage headline
- The church name above that headline
- The Sunday and Wednesday times under Location & Services
- A ministry page title, and the line under that title

Other details are in the form even when they are not clickable on the page. Scroll the form to find them.

## Church details

Open **Church details**. This is the homepage and the facts used across the site.

| What you want to change | Where it is in the form |
| --- | --- |
| The large homepage headline | **Headline** |
| Sunday or Wednesday time | **Gatherings** |
| Kids ages, midday prayer, Wednesday dinner | **Gatherings** |
| This week's message title, series, or speaker | **This week's message** |
| Bulletin | **Bulletin** |
| Address, phone, or email | **Address**, **Phone**, **Office email** |
| Men's study blurb | **Men** |

Times use a 24-hour clock. `09:00` is 9:00 AM. `11:00` is 11:00 AM. `19:00` is 7:00 PM.

The homepage video card, the one that says Watch now, plays the sermon from Subsplash. Changing **Message title** here does not change that card. Upload the sermon in the Subsplash dashboard, the same as today.

## A new bulletin

The bulletin box is a path, not an upload button. The file name has to stay exact, including `-pdf`.

Example: `/files/Bulletin-08-16-26-pdf.pdf`

1. Ask Aaron to put the PDF in the site files with that exact name.
2. In **Church details**, under **Bulletin**, set **PDF path** to that name.
3. Click **Save**.

## Ministry pages

Open **Ministry pages**, then the page you need, such as Women or Students.

You can click the title and the line under it. Paragraphs, scripture, and facts are in the form beside the page.

Words like `{{sunday}}` and `{{email}}` fill in from Church details. Leave them in the sentence. They become the real time or email on the site.

Leave the page's web address alone. Renaming the file would change the public link.

## Save

Click **Save** when the preview looks right.

During this setup, Save keeps the change on this computer. It does not put the new words on the public site. Tell Aaron what you changed, and he publishes it.

If a sentence looks wrong after you type, change it back before you save. If you already saved and need the old words back, tell Aaron. Do not start a second copy of the same edit.

## Leave these alone

- Sermon video and audio. Those are uploaded in Subsplash.
- Online giving and the church app. Those stay in Subsplash.
- **Wallet address**, **Subsplash media**, and **Podcasts**. Those are IDs the site needs. Do not edit them.
- **Show Israel 2027** and **Show VBS 2026**. Leave them off until the church gives real dates.
- **Page switches**. Those turn whole pages on or off.
- Colors, layout, and new pages. Ask Aaron.

## If you get stuck

Stop typing. Note which page you were on, Church details or a ministry page. Tell Aaron. Do not delete a page. The form does not offer a delete button for these pages. If you see one, do not use it.
